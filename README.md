VRK MOD
====

Silently moves the Minecraft Vanilla resource pack to the top.

This mod can effectively prevent some resource pack authors from causing 
their users to mistakenly use Vanilla resource packs to overwrite custom 
resource packs due to improper descriptions, thus making the custom resource packs invalid.

Only works in Fabric/Quilt, will not be ported to Forge or NeoForge, you have to maintain a fork yourself.
